# Personal site

The personal website of Ekin Başar Gökçe at ekinbasargokce.github.io. One home page introduces an engineer-first, researcher-second person and a separate creative world; deeper material lives on its own pages.

## Language

### Structure

**Section**:
A titled block on the home page, reachable from the anchor navigation. Sections are short; a section may link to exactly one Page that holds its long form.
_Avoid_: part, block, panel, tab

**Page**:
A standalone HTML document at its own URL that reuses the shared shell (nav, footer, theme). A page may stand alone or be the long form of one Section.
_Avoid_: subpage, route, view

**Hero**:
The opening of the home page: name, one-line identity (engineer first), link row, and the Doodle.

**Doodle**:
The hand-drawn face in the Hero, drawn by the sketch-face engine from the previous site. A new face is drawn on every visit; it is a playful stand-in for a portrait, never a likeness.
_Avoid_: avatar, portrait, illustration

### Halves of the home page

**Work**:
The professional half of the home page: the Experience, Projects, Research, Education and Tools sections, in that order of emphasis (engineer first, research second).
_Avoid_: professional, career, serious part

**Experience**:
A dated role at an organisation, paid or research: Beespenser, graduate and undergraduate research positions, internships. Two roles at one organisation (Full Stack Engineer, then Lead Developer at Beespenser) are one Experience with two roles.
_Avoid_: job, position, work (Work is the half)

**Project**:
A thing Ekin built, shown with what it does and a link. A Project may come out of an Experience but is listed on its own.
_Avoid_: portfolio item, work

**Research**:
The Section about current research interests (generative drug design) and, later, publications. Research positions held are Experiences, not Research.
_Avoid_: research experience (for this section)

**Off the clock**:
The single Section that holds the creative half. It is one nav item and contains several Strands.
_Avoid_: beyond work, hobbies, studio, play

**Strand**:
One creative practice inside Off the clock, with its own sub-heading and chip in the section's sub-nav. The strands are Photography, Stage, Music, Video and Communities.
_Avoid_: hobby, sub-section, category

### Strands

**Photography**:
The strand for photographs taken by Ekin and the darkroom work at AFT (Amatör Fotoğrafçılık Topluluğu, METU).

**Stage**:
The strand for musical theatre with The Company Musicals (METU) as actor, assistant director and director.
_Avoid_: theatre, drama

**Music**:
The strand for the band (Instagram, videos) and private singing lessons.

**Video**:
The strand for videography: shooting, editing and colour grading the band's videos in DaVinci Resolve, with YouTube references.
_Avoid_: videography (as a heading), film

**Communities**:
The strand for student communities: Biyogen (METU Biology and Genetics community) and hosting the AKEK Evolution Conference three times. AFT and Company Musicals are mentioned here but described in their own strands.
_Avoid_: involvement, clubs, activities

### Pages

**Now**:
A Page with a dated, short account of what Ekin is focused on at the moment, across work and Off the clock.

**Note**:
One dated piece of writing on the Notes Page. The Notes Page may be empty.
_Avoid_: post, blog entry, article

## Flagged ambiguities

- "Research" meant both the research positions held and the research interests. Resolution: positions are Experiences; the Research Section is interests and publications only.
- "Communities" vs the other strands: AFT and Company Musicals are communities too. Resolution: each strand owns its own story; Communities only lists Biyogen/AKEK in full and cross-links the rest.
